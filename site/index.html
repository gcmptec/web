Questions security companies ask

What does it cost after the pilot?

BWP 15–25 per user per month wholesale, depending on volume. You set your own retail — most partners price between BWP 50 and 80.

Does it replace our control room?

No — it feeds it. Your dispatchers keep dispatching; they just stop interrogating panicked callers to find out what's happening and where.

What do our clients get?

One button in the GCMP app. A three-second hold sends audio and location. Dispatch and response stay yours, under your brand relationship.

What about false alarms?

The hold-to-send gate stops pocket presses, and every signal arrives with classification and audio attached — your operator verifies in seconds instead of calling back.

Where does the data live?

On Google Cloud (Firebase and Cloud Run), encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access. Signal data is used for dispatch and audit, not resold.

What is the Discreet tier?

A paid add-on for situations where visibly using a phone isn't safe — covert triggers with a confirmation haptic only the user notices.
