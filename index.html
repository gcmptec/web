Questions security companies ask

What does it cost after the pilot?

A monthly fee per user, quoted on the intro call and depending on volume, with a minimum three-month term. You set your own retail price and keep the margin.

Does it replace our control room?

No — it feeds it. Your dispatchers keep dispatching; they just stop interrogating panicked callers to find out what's happening and where.

What do our clients get?

One button in the GCMP app. A 1.5-second hold sends audio and location. Dispatch and response stay yours, under your brand relationship.

What about false alarms?

The hold-to-send gate stops pocket presses, and every alert arrives with a suggested type and audio attached, so your operator verifies in seconds instead of calling back. Your responder records whether each one was real, so you see your actual false-alarm rate.

Where does the data live?

On Google Cloud (Firebase and Cloud Run), encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access. Data roles, hosting and deletion terms are set out in a written Data Processing Agreement. Your data is never resold.

Does the AI make the call?

No. It suggests an emergency type; your dispatcher decides and dispatches. The responder's recorded outcome shows how often the suggestion was right.

What is the Discreet tier?

A paid add-on for situations where visibly using a phone isn't safe — covert triggers with a confirmation haptic only the user notices.
